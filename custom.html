How customizing works

Three quick steps from your pick to your personalized kit.

1

Choose your jersey

Browse club kits, national teams, and fan favorites. Any jersey in the shop can be customized.

2

At checkout, switch on Add name and/or Add number for that jersey and enter only the details you want.

3

What you can customize

Name on the back

Use up to 15 letters, numbers, or spaces. Your name is checked at checkout before the order is placed.

Jersey number

Choose any number from 0 to 99. We check the number with your order so there is no guesswork.

Simple custom fee

Customization costs ₦3,000 per customized jersey. The fee is shown in your cart and checkout total.

Good to know

Can I customize any jersey?

Yes. Choose any jersey in the shop, then add a name, a number, or both at checkout.

How much does customization cost?

Customization is ₦3,000 for each customized jersey in your cart.

Can I add only a name or only a number?

Yes. Name and number are separate options, so you can add either one or both.

What are the name and number limits?

Names can use up to 15 letters, numbers, or spaces. Jersey numbers can be from 0 to 99.

How are my details confirmed?

The details you add are included with your order. Our team confirms them with you on WhatsApp.

Can I change custom details after ordering?

Changes are only possible before confirmation. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can if you need help.
